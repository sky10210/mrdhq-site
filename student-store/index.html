Project folder 01

Staff Student Design Product Survey

Individual assignmentMarket researchProduct testing

Each student builds an original survey to learn which Chambersburg merchandise designs and products staff might purchase. Research authentic merchandise at the BSN Sideline Store, choose three design options, and test a survey before submitting the link to Mr. D.

The business question: What CASHS merchandise would staff realistically purchase, which design do they prefer, and at what price? Liking a design is not the same as committing to buy.
Open BSN Sideline Store ↗Create Google Form ↗Explore Mentimeter ↗

Your assignment

  1. Find Chambersburg Area Senior High School in the BSN Sideline Store and explore its available design and product options.
  2. Select three genuinely different designs and capture clear screenshots of each. Label them Design A, B, and C.
  3. Build your own 10-question staff survey using Google Forms. Include design images and a variety of question formats.
  4. Use unbiased wording. Ask about product interest, preferred designs, price limits, and realistic likelihood of purchase.
  5. Preview and complete your survey yourself, then have a peer test it. Fix unclear questions before submission.
  6. Submit your working respondent link through the class submission method specified by Mr. D. Do not distribute your individual survey to staff.

Survey introduction: The CASHS Student Store is researching new Chambersburg merchandise options for staff. Your feedback will help our student business team evaluate designs, products, and potential pricing. This anonymous survey takes approximately three minutes and is not an order or commitment to purchase.

Card 04 · Employee handbook

Expectations & Roles

  1. Arrive at the scheduled time, sign in and out using only your own lunch number, and report absences.
  2. Follow the published duties and teacher instructions; report incomplete tasks before leaving.
  3. Welcome customers, protect privacy, and represent CASHS professionally.
  4. Use Square only with approved individual permissions; no shared credentials or unauthorized discounts, refunds, or cash handling.
  5. Protect inventory, keep displays clean and safe, and report discrepancies and mistakes promptly.
  6. Ask for teacher authorization before changing assigned shifts or handling exceptional customer situations.

Cashier / Customer Service

Greet customers, assist with approved checkout, and refer exceptions to the supervisor.

Merchandising / Sales Floor

Organize displays, sizes, price signs, and store presentation.

Inventory / Fulfillment

Count stock, organize deliveries, prepare orders, and report low quantities.

Marketing / Outreach

Create promotions, conduct approved surveys, and help with community projects.

Student Store Participation Agreement

CASHS Student Store · Student employee agreement

By participating in the student store, I agree to follow my assigned schedule, log my own attendance accurately, perform assigned work, serve customers professionally, and follow all applicable school rules and supervising-teacher directions.

I understand that merchandise, cash, payment equipment, account credentials, and customer information must be protected. I will not use another person's sign-in, alter records, process unauthorized refunds or discounts, or remove store property. I will report errors, safety concerns, and suspected discrepancies promptly to the supervising teacher.

I understand that store work is a supervised educational opportunity. My duties and register access depend on completed training, school requirements, and teacher approval. If expectations are not met, the teacher may reassign duties, require retraining, or suspend store responsibilities in accordance with applicable school procedures.

I understand that attendance and assigned-task records may be reviewed by authorized school staff for educational program management. The final agreement will be reviewed for school policy and any required family consent before use.

Signature fields for final approved version: Student printed name, student signature, date, supervising teacher signature, and parent/guardian acknowledgment if required by CASD.

Informal student and parent/guardian typed acknowledgments are recorded by the store website with server timestamps.
