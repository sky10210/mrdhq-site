Staff Student Design Product Survey

Individual assignmentMarket researchProduct testing

Each student builds an original survey to learn which Chambersburg merchandise designs and products staff might purchase. Research authentic merchandise at the BSN Sideline Store, choose three design options, and test a survey before submitting the link to Mr. D.

The business question: What CASHS merchandise would staff realistically purchase, which design do they prefer, and at what price? Liking a design is not the same as committing to buy.
Open BSN Sideline Store ↗Create Google Form ↗Explore Mentimeter ↗

Your assignment

  1. Find Chambersburg Area Senior High School in the BSN Sideline Store and explore its available design and product options.
  2. Select three genuinely different designs and capture clear screenshots of each. Label them Design A, B, and C.
  3. Build your own 10-question staff survey using Google Forms. Include design images and a variety of question formats.
  4. Use unbiased wording. Ask about product interest, preferred designs, price limits, and realistic likelihood of purchase.
  5. Preview and complete your survey yourself, then have a peer test it. Fix unclear questions before submission.
  6. Submit your working respondent link through the class submission method specified by Mr. D. Do not distribute your individual survey to staff.

Survey introduction: The CASHS Student Store is researching new Chambersburg merchandise options for staff. Your feedback will help our student business team evaluate designs, products, and potential pricing. This anonymous survey takes approximately three minutes and is not an order or commitment to purchase.

Student checklist

☐ Three actual BSN design images
☐ All ten questions
☐ Appropriate response types
☐ Price question tied to a specific product
☐ Clear, unbiased wording
☐ No unnecessary personal information
☐ Self-test and peer-test completed
☐ Respondent link submitted

Class process: Students build individual research instruments; the store team combines the strongest questions into one official staff-facing survey for distribution.
