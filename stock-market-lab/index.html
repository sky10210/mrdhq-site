STOCK MARKET 101

Five ideas before your first pick.

01

Stock

A stock represents partial ownership in a company.

02

Share price

The market price of one share. A higher price does not automatically mean a better company.

03

Portfolio

The collection of investments you own.

04

Diversification

Spreading investments across different businesses and industries rather than relying on one.

05

Return

The gain or loss on an investment compared with what you originally invested.
