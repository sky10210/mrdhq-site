How does a business become something people can invest in?

Start with a person who has an idea for a product or service.

1 / 8
01

What is a stock?

A share is a small piece of ownership in a company.

02

Price ≠ company value

A $5 share is not automatically cheaper than a $100 share. The number of shares matters too.

03

Why prices move

Buyers and sellers react to results, news, expectations, products, competition and the economy.

04

Your strategy

Research companies, spread risk across industries, watch what changes and explain your decisions.

05

This simulation

You receive classroom money, choose companies by industry and track how those choices perform. No real money is involved.

06

Where this can go

Over several years, a business may grow, struggle, raise more money, buy competitors or change completely. Investors keep reassessing what its future may be worth.

DON'T GET FOOLED BY SHARE PRICE

Which company is worth more?

Company A1 million shares × $100$100 million company value
Company B100 million shares × $5$500 million company value

Company B has the lower stock price but the larger market value. One share's price alone does not tell you how valuable the whole company is.
