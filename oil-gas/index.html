ELI5

Why doesn’t gas move exactly with oil?

Brent is a global benchmark for crude oil. Gasoline prices also include refining, transportation, taxes, local competition and fuel that stations already purchased. That is why pump prices may react later—or by a different amount.

Crude oil + refining + transportation + taxes + local competition = pump price
Correlation warning

Two prices moving together does not prove that one event caused the entire change.
