What is Brent crude?

Brent is one of the world's main benchmark oil prices. Think of it as a widely watched global price tag for a barrel of crude oil. When Brent changes, it affects what refiners may pay for future supplies—but the price at your gas station usually does not change at the exact same time.

Crude oil + refining + transportation + taxes + local competition = pump price
What moves the market?
1
SupplyProduction cuts, war, sanctions, pipelines, shipping and refinery outages.
2
DemandTravel, economic growth, seasons and how much fuel consumers use.
3
ExpectationsTraders react to what they think could happen next, not only what happened today.
