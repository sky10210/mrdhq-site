Discussion 2 • Stock Market Basics

Pick Your Stock

Choose a company or brand and organize the basic information you will use for your stock infographic.

  1. Company or Brand: What company/brand did you choose?
  2. Parent Company: Does another company own it? If yes, identify the parent company.
  3. Ticker Symbol: What ticker would an investor search?
  4. What They Sell: What are the company's main products or services?
  5. Major Brands: List 2–3 recognizable brands/products connected to the company.
  6. Current Stock Price: What is one share currently trading for? Include the date you checked it.
