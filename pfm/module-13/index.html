Teacher notes: Pennsylvania standards connections

This module extends the auto-insurance sequence with introductory practice in Pennsylvania's Risk and Insurance standards. It supports continued instruction; one module does not establish mastery of the whole strand.

  • 17.5.9-12.A–B: Identify financial exposure and compare risk reduction, savings, and transfer (Q1, Q16, Q20).
  • 17.5.9-12.C: Match renters, homeowners, health, life, and disability coverage to needs (Q1–11, Q20).
  • 17.5.9-12.D–E: Use provider resources, compare protection terms, calculate costs, and outline claims steps (Q6, Q14–17, Q20).
  • 17.5.9-12.F: Recognize lease and mortgage proof-of-coverage requirements (Q2, Q4).
  • 17.5.9-12.G: Introduce the role and limits of public protections (Q12–13).
