What kind of college are we talking about?

Community college and state university describe school types. Division I (D1) describes an athletics classification; D1 schools can be public or private, and D1 does not guarantee athletic aid. An out-of-state public university typically charges nonresident rates. Sticker price and net price after grants can differ greatly.

ROI means more than a salary number

Think about money spent, earnings you delay while training, career growth, benefits, and how likely you are to finish. An apprenticeship earns money during training; college may open jobs that require degrees; cosmetology or barbering can lead to employment or business ownership.
