Pennsylvania State Police Trooper

Patrol highways and communities, investigate crashes, respond to emergencies and enforce laws. No college degree is required: a high school diploma or GED qualifies academically. Applicants must be 20 to apply and 21–39 at appointment, meet citizenship and licensing rules, and pass competitive testing, background, fitness, medical and psychological screening. Selected cadets complete approximately 28 weeks of paid academy training. This is a future goal for most recent high school graduates, not an immediate 18-year-old entry job.
