The Meta Case • Aug. 26, 2026

Who is responsible for keeping teens from getting hooked?

Meta, the company behind Instagram and Facebook, reached settlements with U.S. states after allegations that its platforms were designed to keep young users engaged and that the company misled the public about risks. Meta denies wrongdoing.

What changed?

2-hour daily limitTeen Facebook and Instagram use would be limited to two hours per day unless a parent allows more.
Midnight–6 a.m. blockTeen use would be blocked overnight unless a parent gives permission.
School-hour quietMost push notifications would be disabled from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m.
More teen protectionsThe agreement includes stronger age checks, hidden likes/reactions by default, and an option for a non-personalized feed.
