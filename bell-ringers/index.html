Teacher Setup

Build a quick partner warm-up before students arrive. Everything you type here stays hidden until the correct point in the activity.

How this Bell Ringer works
  1. Enter a topic students can discuss without much setup.
  2. Add a strong/model response that will stay covered until after discussion.
  3. Add one deeper question or takeaway to close the warm-up.
  4. Choose a timer, click Ready for Class, then project full screen.
  5. Students play best-of-three first. Only after Results do winners learn they are the speakers.
Example: Topic: “Would you rather have $1,000 today or $150 a month for a year? Why?”   Strong response: compare the immediate $1,000 with the $1,800 total.   Takeaway: decisions involve timing, value, risk, and opportunity cost.
