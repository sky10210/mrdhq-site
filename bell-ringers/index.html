Teacher Guide

Rock Paper Scissors is a quick think → talk → share routine. The game assigns the speaker without students knowing the role ahead of time.

Before class

  1. Open Setup and enter one short prompt with more than one defensible answer.
  2. Choose Think Time and Talk Time.
  3. Add a Strong Response, Dig Deeper question, and Lesson Connection if useful.
  4. Project the page and use Full Screen.

Run the activity

  1. Students find a shoulder partner and play best-of-three Rock Paper Scissors.
  2. After everyone finishes, click Results. Winners become speakers; partners become listeners.
  3. Reveal the prompt, then give silent Think Time so every student forms an answer.
  4. Move to Talk. Speaker explains first; listener asks a question or adds an idea.
  5. On Share, ask students to report their partner’s thinking.
  6. Reveal the Strong Response only after students have committed to their own reasoning.
  7. Use Dig Deeper/Connect to bridge into the lesson, then finish on Ready.

Teacher controls

Space = next stage   •   T = start/pause timer   •   R = reset timer. The timer controls also work by clicking.
