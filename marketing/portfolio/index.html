Create a free Neocities account

Go to Neocities and choose a professional username. That username becomes part of your public portfolio address.

Open your website files

Your Neocities dashboard is basically the folder that holds your website.

Find index.html

This is your homepage file. Open it in the Neocities editor.

Open the Code Lab on this page

The Code Lab contains your starter portfolio HTML. You can edit and preview it before publishing.

Personalize the starter

Change YOUR NAME, rewrite About Me, update your services, and customize your first project card.

Preview before publishing

Click Update Preview in the Code Lab. Check that the page still works and looks professional.

Copy your finished HTML

Click Copy HTML. Return to Neocities, select the old contents of index.html, and paste your new code.

Save and view your live site

Save index.html, open your Neocities website, and refresh. Your portfolio is now online.

ELI5: What is index.html?

Imagine your website is a store. Your Neocities account is the building. The files inside it are the rooms, signs, and displays. index.html is the front door.

When somebody visits yourname.neocities.org, the browser asks, “What page should I show first?” It looks for index.html. That is why your homepage is normally named index.html.
