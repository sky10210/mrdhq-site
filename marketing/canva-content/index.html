Design the communication

Canva is our common classroom tool for social graphics and short videos. Harry Guinness’s September 2026 Zapier review selects Canva for template-based social design and Adobe Express for another workflow. That is an independent reviewer’s assessment, not proof that every professional uses the same tool. Specialist designers and video editors also use tools such as Photoshop, Illustrator, and Premiere Pro.

Today’s workflow: audience → message → layout → post → video → review. Keep Canva open in another tab while you practice. The module itself needs no Google sign-in; Canva may require its own account.

Free path: original uploads, basic shapes/text, manual cropping, a brand reference page, manual timing, and timed text captions. Use school-account features if available; purchasing an upgrade is not part of this assignment.

Final Canva Challenge

Fictional brief: Summit Scoops is launching Cherry Mango Swirl on Friday, October 9, 2026, from 4–7 PM at a school-approved mock pop-up. A pint costs $5. Audience: students looking for an after-school treat. Goal: explain the flavor and invite a visit. Treat every detail as a classroom simulation.

SUMMIT SCOOPS

WOW! AMAZING! NEW!

Ice cream ice cream ice cream

Cherry Mango Swirl • $5 • Friday Oct. 9 • 4–7 PM • mock pop-up

Check us out!!!

Rebuild this post

The logo dominates, the message is generic, and the useful details are too small. Rebuild it in Canva using your hand-drawn pint or an original classroom product image. Keep the facts, but improve the communication.

  1. Create a 1080 × 1080 PNG/JPG with a clear flavor/offer, readable launch details, consistent branding, and one CTA.
  2. Create a 1080 × 1920, 15-second MP4 for the same brand. Use a hook, product/benefit, and CTA. Add checked captions if there is speech, or readable timed text if there is no speech.
  3. Show both to a partner at phone size. Ask what is offered and what to do. Make one revision based on their feedback.

Before/after target: Instead of “WOW! AMAZING! NEW!”, make “Cherry Mango Swirl • $5” the main offer and “Try it Friday, Oct. 9 · 4–7 PM · mock pop-up” the action. Your actual layout is your decision.

Submit: Upload the final static post to the existing Marketing Project Gallery. Show Mr. D your exported MP4 or submit it through the teacher’s designated class assignment. This module saves your quiz and written explanation, not video files.

Quality checklist: audience fit · true facts · readable type · clear hierarchy · consistent brand · clean crop/layers · purposeful motion · checked captions/audio · correct export.

In the response below, name your audience and main action; explain three design decisions; describe one change you made after feedback; and identify where you submitted the post and video. You may add a view-only Canva link if your teacher requests one. Your hands-on work is reviewed separately from the 20-question score.
