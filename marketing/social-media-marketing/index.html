From making content to using it

Module 6 taught you to create a strong post and a short video. Now decide who should see them, where they belong, what they should accomplish, and how to judge the result. Start with customer evidence, not your favorite app.

Attention

Help relevant people discover the offer.

Engagement

Give them something useful to watch, discuss, save, or explore.

Action

Make the next step clear and measure whether it happens.

Professional perspective: Sprout Social’s 2026 research surveyed consumers and marketers and emphasizes tailoring content to platform behavior, retaining human creativity, and supporting customers. These findings inform the lesson; they do not guarantee results for a specific business.

Today: 20 mini-lessons and scenario questions, followed by a short strategy plan. All examples and performance figures are fictional classroom scenarios. You do not need to create a social account, buy an ad, or publish publicly.

Final Canva Challenge: 3-Slide Social Media Plan

You are now the social media marketer. Create a professional 3-slide Canva presentation that you could show to the owner of the brand. Your plan should clearly answer: Who are we trying to reach? What are we trying to accomplish? What three posts will we make? How will we know whether they worked?

Choose your brand: You may use your Module 6 ice cream brand, your own business idea, or your own social media/creator channel. Whatever you choose must fit the three-post strategy below.

SLIDE 1 — THE STRATEGY

This is your owner-facing overview.

  • Brand, business, or channel name
  • Target audience
  • What that audience wants or needs
  • Primary social platform
  • Why that platform fits the audience
  • Main goal: awareness, engagement, website traffic, store visits, sales, followers, or another appropriate objective
  • One measurable goal or metric
SLIDE 2 — THE THREE-POST PLAN

Show three clear post cards or mockups across the slide. Do not simply make three advertisements. Each post needs a different purpose.

  1. POST 1 — HELP: Helpful/educational. Include what viewers will see, hook/headline, CTA, and metric.
  2. POST 2 — CONNECT: Behind-the-scenes/community. Include what viewers will see, hook/headline, CTA, and metric.
  3. POST 3 — SELL: Promotional. Include what viewers will see, hook/headline, CTA, and metric.
SLIDE 3 — HOW WE'LL KNOW IF IT WORKED
  • What metric matters most?
  • What would count as success?
  • Which post do you predict will perform best, and why?
  • How would you adapt one post for a second platform?
  • What would you change if the results were weak?
  • Next action: repeat, revise, or stop?
SAMPLE ONLY — DO NOT COPY

SUMMIT SCOOPS

Slide 1 — Strategy

Audience: CASHS students looking for an inexpensive after-school treat.

Platform: Instagram

Goal: Get students to visit Friday's mock pop-up.

Measure: Reach, saves, and visits connected to the advertised offer.

Sample Slide 2 — Three Posts

HELP: “What makes Cherry Mango Swirl?” Short Reel showing the flavor ingredients. CTA: “Save this if you'd try it.” Metric: saves.

CONNECT: “Making Friday's first batch.” Behind-the-scenes Reel showing preparation. CTA: “Which flavor should we make next?” Metric: comments.

SELL: Hero image of the pint with “Cherry Mango Swirl • $5 • Friday 4–7 PM.” CTA: “Stop by Friday and try it.” Metric: reach plus visits.

Sample Slide 3 — Measurement

Primary metric: store visits. Supporting metrics: reach, saves, and comments. Prediction: the behind-the-scenes Reel will get the most engagement because it gives viewers something new instead of only advertising. TikTok adaptation: faster opening, vertical video, product shown immediately, shorter text. If results are weak: test a stronger opening hook and a different posting time.

Remember: Summit Scoops is a guiding example only. Use your own details, wording, visuals, and strategy.

Submit: Build all three slides in Canva, export the presentation as one PDF, and upload the finished Social Media Plan to the Marketing Project Gallery / Project Files. There is no written reflection for this final project.
