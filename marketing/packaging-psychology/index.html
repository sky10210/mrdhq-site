From shopper to packaging designer

Think about how a package gets noticed, helps a shopper understand a product, and protects the item. Then consider the business side: material, production, shipping, profit and sustainability. Finally, prepare two related seasonal packages for your Brand Expo.

Project connection: Choose cologne/perfume, lip gloss or another beauty product. Design a light summer edition and a darker winter edition under one recognizable brand. You may later create a five-item discovery set.
