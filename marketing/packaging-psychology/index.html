From shopper to packaging designer

Think about how a package gets noticed, helps a shopper understand a product, and protects the item. Then consider the business side: material, production, shipping, profit and sustainability. Finally, explore ideas you could use in a future packaging or branding project.

Project connection idea: As you learn, think about a product you might want to create packaging for someday. Who would buy it? What would make its package recognizable, useful, and affordable? You are brainstorming only—the final class project has not been announced.
